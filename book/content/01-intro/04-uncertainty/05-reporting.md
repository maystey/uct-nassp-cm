\begin{frame}{Reporting Uncertainty}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item All information needed to re-evaluate the measurement should be available.
        \item If quoting expanded uncertainty, confidence must be quoted as well.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

%From specific guidance
\begin{frame}{Reporting Uncertainty}{Quoting the Results}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Quoting the measurement result $y$ of measurand $Y$ with standard uncertainty $u(y)$
    \item If $u(y)$ is a combined uncertainty, this should be stated in the quote directly (if not mentioned earlier in your report)
    \item A good practice is to quote the uncertainty to 2 significant figures, and quote the best approximation of the measurement to match this.
    \item For example, a mass $m = 100.021 47$g with uncertainty $u = 0.35$ mg.
    \item Some of the options are:
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item $m = 100.021 47$g with $u = 0.35$ mg
        \item $m = 100.021 47(35)$g  where the number in parentheses is the numerical value of $u$ referred to the corresponding last digits of the quoted result.
        \item $m = 100.021 47(0. 000 35)$g where the number in parentheses is the numerical value of  $u$ expressed in the unit of the quoted result.
        \item $m = 100.021 47 \pm 0. 000 35$g  where the number following the symbol $\pm$ is the numerical value of $u$ and not a confidence interval.
    \end{enumerate}
\end{itemize}


\end{frame}


\begin{frame}{Reporting Uncertainty}{Quoting the Results}
    
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Don't forget to quote the coverage factor and level of confidence after the measurand and expanded uncertainty. 
        \item For example, a combined standard uncertainty quoted with a coverage factor of 2:\\
        ``The reported uncertainty is an expanded uncertainty calculated using a coverage factor k = 2, providing a confidence level of approximately $95\%$. The combined standard uncertainty was determined in accordance with the principles of the Guide to the Expression of Uncertainty in Measurement (GUM).''
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
